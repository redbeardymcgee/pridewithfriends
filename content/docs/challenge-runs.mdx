## Slay the Spire 2 challenge runs

We've prepared a small list of silly suggestions for challenge runs you can do as part of <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> this year. These are intended to add a fun extra element to your runs, and you are welcome to try them as many times as you like. Perhaps set them as donation incentives for your individual campaign in the fundraiser!

<Tabs>
  <Tab value="Pride's Bane">
    Replace `Ascenders Bane` with the <PrideGradient>Pride</PrideGradient> curse from Spire 1 Endless mode!

    This should provide a noticeable difficulty increase, as the <PrideGradient>Pride</PrideGradient> curse is not `Ethereal`. It duplicates itself on top of your draw pile at the end of your turn if you don't spend 1 energy to play the card.

    Check out the mod [here](https://github.com/aescastro/PridesBane)!

    <Callout variant="info">
      Don't forget to add the <ExampleText>!pridecurse</ExampleText> bot command! Check out the instructions [here](/docs/chatbot-commands).
    </Callout>

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Get Glamourous">
    Add as many `Glam` enchanted cards as possible to your deck!
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Luxury Gay Space">
    Find the card `Royalties`, then obtain as much money as possible. Do NOT spend it; we need that to sustain our <PrideGradient>Luxury Gay Space Community Paradise</PrideGradient>!
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Constellation Partners">
    This is a multiplayer challenge with 3-4 Regents. Play a `Soulbound` coop in which every player must choose options in identical positions.

    This applies to Neow & Ancient blessings, event choices (including Divination!), and shop purchases!

    <Callout variant="tip">
      If the challenge proves too overwhelming, consider limiting the Soulbound selection rule to only card rewards and shop purchases at first.
    </Callout>

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

We hope these sound like enjoyable ways to bring a little queer fun and extra challenge to your <PrideGradient>Pride Month</PrideGradient> runs!
