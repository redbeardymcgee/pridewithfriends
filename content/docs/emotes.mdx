## 7TV Emotes

Add the **Pride With Friends** emotes to your 7TV emote set to use them on your stream and in chat.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Login to your [7tv profile](https://7tv.app).

    <Callout variant="info">
      Don't forget to [download the 7tv extension or compatible apps](https://7tv.app/#download)!
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>
    Navigate to the <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> [emotes](https://7tv.app/users/01FMH1KYZ00001FS6N1BY0V4ZM/uploaded), uploaded by <TutorialText>redbeardymcgee</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click an emote, then <TutorialText><Plus className="inline-block" size={22}/> Emote to [your name]'s Emotes</TutorialText>. Repeat this for each emote that you'd like to use!

    <Callout variant="tip">
      If you'd like to add these to a new Emote set, use the <TutorialText><Folder className="inline-block" size={12} /> Emote Sets</TutorialText> link in the left sidebar. Click <TutorialText><Plus className="inline-block" size={12}/> Create New Set</TutorialText> in the top right. To add an emote to the new set, use the <TutorialText><Folder className="inline-block" size={12}><Plus size={12} x={6} y={6} absoluteStrokeWidth /></Folder> Add to...</TutorialText> button on the emote's page and assign it to the correct set.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>

The emotes are now available to use in chat and on your stream!

<Callout variant="warning">
  Emotes may take a few minutes to appear in chat after adding them to your set. Try refreshing your browser if you don't see them after a few minutes.
</Callout>
