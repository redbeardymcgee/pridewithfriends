## Chatbot commands

Inviting donations and participation in a charity event from your viewers can be daunting. Here are some commands and timers you may add to your chatbot to help communicate what the event is about and how viewers may engage.

### Commands

Chat commands must be triggered by typing the name. Most are named with a prefixed with an exclamation point like <ExampleText>!helloworld</ExampleText>, but many bots support commands triggered by any or no prefix at all. These are helpful for answering common questions about the event, such as where to donate or learn more about the charity.

### Timers

Timer commands are automatically triggered on a periodic interval, without requiring someone to type a command. These are useful to remind viewers about the event, the charity, and the meaning of Pride.

This year we are supporting Mermaids, an organization that provides phone, email and web chat support, moderated online forums, events and groups for families and young people, training, and campaigning for the things that matter most to trans children and young people.</ExampleText>

</Tabs>
