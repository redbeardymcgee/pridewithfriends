## Create overlays

After joining the <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> team and campaign, you will want to add stream overlays to alert yourself and your users to incoming donations. It also enables you to display the progress of your campaign and total donations received already.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dashboard">
    Visit your [Tiltify dashboard](https://app.tiltify.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Campaigns">
    In the left sidebar, click <TutorialText>Campaigns</TutorialText>.

    <Callout variant="tip">
      This page can also be found directly [here](https://app.tiltify.com/fundraiser).
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Your campaign">
    Find and click the campaign you created during [Campaign setup](/docs/campaign-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Branding">
    From this new dashboard, find <TutorialText>Branding</TutorialText> in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Overlays">
    Click <TutorialText>Overlays</TutorialText> in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click <TutorialText>Create an overlay</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A default overlay with all the basic elements is now presented. It includes a classic tracker across the top with a list of recent donations below. In the bottom left you will see a QR code that links directly to your campaign's donation page. In the bottom right, there is an animated alert box.

<Callout variant="tip">
  While this overlay may be used as-is, we strongly encourage you to customize the <TutorialText>Alert Box</TutorialText> to add a cute gif and a sound. This helps ensure that you don't miss incoming donations!
</Callout>

You will need to add this overlay as a browser source in OBS Studio.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy url">
    In the top right, click the <TutorialText>Copy overlay url</TutorialText> button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a browser source">
    In OBS Studio, find the <TutorialText>Sources</TutorialText> panel. Click the plus button and choose <TutorialText>Browser</TutorialText>. Name it something memorable, like <ExampleText>PWF Donations</ExampleText>.

    <Callout variant="tip">
      Consider copying your <TutorialText>Scene Collection</TutorialText> in the top menu. Choose <TutorialText>Duplicate...</TutorialText> and give it a name like <ExampleText>Pride With Friends <CurrentYear /></ExampleText>. This will make it easy to revert to your normal setup after <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> ends.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure source">
    Set the <TutorialText>URL</TutorialText> to the overlay url you copied in step 1 above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Width & height">
    Ensure <TutorialText>Width</TutorialText> is `1920` and <TutorialText>Height</TutorialText> is `1080`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click the <TutorialText>OK</TutorialText> button to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="info">
  You new overlay may be the wrong size for your stream. To fix this, right click it in the <TutorialText>Sources</TutorialText> panel, hover <TutorialText>Transform</TutorialText> and then click <TutorialText>Fit to screen</TutorialText>. You may also click the source to select it, then press `Ctrl+f` to do the same thing.
</Callout>

Your overlay is now ready to use! It will be working right away, so the next time you go live be vigilant for incoming donations. Don't forget to thank everyone who contributes and read any donation message to let them know you're listening.

<Callout variant="danger">
  Exercise caution when reading donation messages! Try to make sure it doesn't say anything suspicious or anti-queer before repeating it on your stream. Trolls are rare, but it's important to remain vigilant.
</Callout>
