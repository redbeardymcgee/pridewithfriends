## Donation Links

Donation links are critical in directing your community to contribute toward the fundraiser.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Visit your at [campaign dashboard](https://app.tiltify.com/hub) then clicking <TutorialText>Campaigns</TutorialText> in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click the campaign you created during [campaign setup](/docs/campaign-setup)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Copy campaign link</TutorialText> for sharing your campaign progress page.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Copy donate link</TutorialText> to send people directly to the contribution page.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Add these links to your chatbot and share them on your socials!

  <Callout variant="info">
    If you need help setting up your chatbot with links, check the <TutorialText>Bot</TutorialText> section in the sidebar on the left of this page.
  </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>
