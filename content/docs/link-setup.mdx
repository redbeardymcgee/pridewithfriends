## Set up your Donation Links!

1. Visit your campaign dashboard by at [https://app.tiltify.com/hub](https://app.tiltify.com/hub) or clicking the menu on the top right of the Tiltify page.
1. Click the campaign you created earlier <ExampleText>Pride With Friends 2025 [YOURNAME]</ExampleText>
1. Use the share or copy buttons to get a valid link
   - <TutorialText>Share</TutorialText>
   - <TutorialText>Copy Donate URL</TutorialText>
   - <TutorialText>Copy Campaign URL</TutorialText>.
1. Using your Twitch channel chatbot of choice, add or edit your `!donate`/`!pwf`/`!pride` command to include the donate URL copied from the <TutorialText>Share</TutorialText> panel.
