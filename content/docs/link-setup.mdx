## Set up your Donation Links!

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open dashboard">
    <p>Visit your campaign dashboard by at [https://app.tiltify.com/hub](https://app.tiltify.com/hub) or clicking the menu on the top right of the Tiltify page.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select campaign">
    <p>Click the campaign you created earlier <ExampleText>Pride With Friends 2025 [YOURNAME]</ExampleText></p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a link">
    <p>Use the share or copy buttons to get a valid link</p>
    <ul>
      <li><TutorialText>Share</TutorialText></li>
      <li><TutorialText>Copy Donate URL</TutorialText></li>
      <li><TutorialText>Copy Campaign URL</TutorialText>.</li>
    </ul>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to chatbot">
    <p>Using your Twitch channel chatbot of choice, add or edit your `!donate`/`!pwf`/`!pride` command to include the donate URL copied from the <TutorialText>Share</TutorialText> panel.</p>
  </Step>
</Steps>
