## Campaign Setup

Now that you are a member of the team, you will be able to automatically add your own fundraiser to the overall campaign!

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login and join">
    Ensure you are logged into your Tiltify account, then click [this link](https://start.tiltify.com?supportingFactId=34a3bb09-ed1e-4930-9f0b-348723ad829d).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quick Start">
    Click <TutorialText>Skip with Quick Start</TutorialText> to choose how you will share the campaign with donors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select sharing options">
    Select <TutorialText>Sharing on Social</TutorialText> and <TutorialText>Live Streaming</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click <TutorialText>Confirm</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decline other creators">
    Select <TutorialText>No</TutorialText> when it suggests to allow other creators to support your fundraising effort. It should look like the below image.

    <Callout variant="tip" title="Invite others to join">
       Consider inviting other creators to join through the link in the first step above!
    </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame align="center">
  <Image src="/images/docs/create-campaign.png" alt="Create campaign wizard demonstrating the correct selections" />
</Frame>

After confirming, you will be offered an opportunity to edit a couple more details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your campaign">
    Please edit the <TutorialText>Campaign name</TutorialText> to clarify that it is a <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> fundraiser.

    <Callout variant="tip">
      Most creators will use a name like <ExampleText>Pride With Friends 2026 [YOURNAME]</ExampleText>.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your campaign">
    Feel free to add your own description in <TutorialText>About your campaign</TutorialText>.

    <Callout variant="tip">
      Make sure to mention the charity being supported by this year's event!
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Donation goal">
    Set the <TutorialText>Goal</TutorialText> to any value you feel is both comfortable and achievable.

    <Callout variant="note">
      There is no obligation to meet your goal; every dollar goes toward and amazing cause!
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    You may now <TutorialText>Save & publish</TutorialText> to officially launch your <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> fundraiser!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame align="center">
  <Image src="/images/docs/review-publish.png" alt="Final details editor before publishing" />
</Frame>

You will now be at the Campaign Dashboard for <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient>. This is where you may edit additional details of the campaign to increase the chance of reaching your donation goal.
