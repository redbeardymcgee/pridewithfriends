## Campaign Setup

Now that you are a member of the team, you will be able to automatically add your own fundraiser to the overall campaign!

1. Ensure you are logged into your Tiltify account, then click [this link](https://start.tiltify.com?supportingFactId=34a3bb09-ed1e-4930-9f0b-348723ad829d).
1. Click <TutorialText>Skip with Quick Start</TutorialText> to choose how you will share the campaign with donors.
1. Select <TutorialText>Sharing on Social</TutorialText> and <TutorialText>Live Streaming</TutorialText>.
1. <TutorialText>Confirm</TutorialText> your choice
1. Select <TutorialText>No</TutorialText> when it suggests to allow other creators to support your fundraising effort. They will already be following these same steps to support the <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> campaign. It should look like the below image.

<Frame align="center">
  <Image src="/images/docs/create-campaign.png" alt="Create campaign wizard demonstrating the correct selections" />
</Frame>

After confirming, you will be offered an opportunity to edit a couple more details.

1. Please edit the <TutorialText>Campaign name</TutorialText> to clarify that it is a <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> fundraiser. Most other participants will name it <ExampleText>Pride With Friends 2026 [YOURNAME]</ExampleText>).
1. Feel free to add your own description in <TutorialText>About your campaign</TutorialText>, but ensure that the Mermaids charity we are supporting is mentioned!
1. Set the <TutorialText>Goal</TutorialText> to any value you feel is both comfortable and achievable.
1. You may now <TutorialText>Save & publish</TutorialText> to officially launch your <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient> fundraiser!

<Frame align="center">
  <Image src="/images/docs/review-publish.png" alt="Final details editor before publishing" />
</Frame>

You will now be at the Campaign Dashboard for <PrideGradient>Pride With Friends</PrideGradient>. This is where you may edit additional details of the campaign to increase the chance of reaching your donation goal.
