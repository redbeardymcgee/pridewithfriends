## Nightbot

[Nightbot](https://docs.nightbot.tv/) is an extremely popular free chatbot for multiple platforms. Get started by logging into your [Nightbot Dashboard](https://nightbot.tv/dashboard).

### Commands

<Steps>
  <Step>
    On the left sidebar, click <TutorialText>Commands</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>New Command</TutorialText> in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Name the command.

    <Callout variant="warning">
      Use a memorable name, and don't forget to include the command trigger prefix!
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>
    In <TutorialText>Response</TutorialText>, paste a command message from [Chatbot commands](/docs/chatbot-commands). Feel free to customize it for your community, or create your own.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Leave the <TutorialText>Required User-Level</TutorialText> on the default setting, <TutorialText>Everyone</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Repeat these steps for any extra commands you'd like to add. Consider including them in your stream titles during the event to make viewers aware!

### Timers

<Steps>
  <Step>
    On the left sidebar, click <TutorialText>Timers</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>New Timer</TutorialText> in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Name the timer.

    <Callout variant="info">
      The name of this timer is not strictly important. It will be triggered automatically according to the interval defined below the message.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>
    In <TutorialText>Message</TutorialText> paste a timer message from [Chatbot commands](/docs/chatbot-commands). Feel free to customize it for your community, or create your own.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Set the <TutorialText>Interval</TutorialText> to a value that suits your stream's chat activity.

    <Callout variant="tip">
       The default 5 minutes will probably be too frequent. Try starting with a 15 minute interval; this can always be readjusted later.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>

This is enough to make Nightbot remind chatters about the charity and event. You may also disable the timer unless a certain number of chat lines have been sent since the previous trigger, or alias the timer to an existing command to reuse the same message. These options may be found by clicking the <TutorialText>Show Advanced Settings</TutorialText> button.
