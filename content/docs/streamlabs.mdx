## Streamlabs Cloudbot

[Streamlabs](https://streamlabs.com) is a platform offering alerts, overlays, a chatbot and more for free. Get started by logging into your [Streamlabs dashboard](https://streamlabs.com/dashboard).

### Commands

<Steps>
  <Step>
    On the left sidebar, click <TutorialText>Cloudbot</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Commands</TutorialText> in the tab bar located near the center.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Add Command</TutorialText> in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Name the command.

    <Callout variant="warning">
      Use a memorable name, and don't forget to include the command trigger prefix!
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>
    In <TutorialText>Response</TutorialText>, paste a command message from [Chatbot commands](/docs/chatbot-commands). Feel free to customize it for your community, or create your own.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Leave the <TutorialText>Reply in</TutorialText> and <TutorialText>Permission</TutorialText> at the default settings.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Confirm</TutorialText> in the bottom left.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="info">
  There are many more options in the <TutorialText>Advanced</TutorialText> tab, but they shouldn't be necessary to modify.
</Callout>

Repeat these steps for any extra commands you'd like to add. Consider including them in your stream titles during the event to make viewers aware!

### Timers

<Steps>
  <Step>
    On the left sidebar, click <TutorialText>Cloudbot</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Timers</TutorialText> in the tab bar located near the center.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Add Timer</TutorialText> in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Name the timer.

    <Callout variant="info">
      The name of this timer is not strictly important. It will be triggered automatically according to the interval defined below.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>
    In <TutorialText>Response</TutorialText> paste a timer message from [Chatbot commands](/docs/chatbot-commands). Feel free to customize it for your community, or create your own.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Set the <TutorialText>Interval</TutorialText> to a value that suits your stream's chat activity.

    <Callout variant="tip">
       The default 5 minutes will probably be too frequent. Try starting with a 15 minute interval; this can always be readjusted later.
    </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>

This is enough to make Streamlabs Cloudbot remind chatters about the charity and event.
