## Donation Panel Setup

<Steps>
  <Step>
    From the Twitch homepage, click your profile icon in the top right to open the options menu.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Channel</TutorialText> to go to your Twitch channel.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click the <TutorialText>About</TutorialText> tab under the video player.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click the <TutorialText>Edit Panels</TutorialText> under the <TutorialText>About Me</TutorialText> section.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click the large gray plus symbol.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Add a Text or Image Panel</TutorialText>.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Add Image</TutorialText>.

    <Callout variant="info">
      Right click the panel image below, and click <TutorialText>Save image as...</TutorialText>.
      <Frame align="center">
        <Image src="/images/docs/pwf-panel.png"/>
      </Frame>
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click the upload box to select the panel image, or drag it over from your file browser.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Fill out the description with a message similar to this, edited to your liking:
    <ExampleText>Donate to Mermaids by clicking this panel!</ExampleText>

    <Callout variant="tip">
      Consider adding an <TutorialText>Accessible Image Description</TutorialText> to enable screen readers to describe this panel!
    </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>
    Fill in the <TutorialText>Image Links to</TutorialText> box with your [donation URL](/docs/link-setup)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click <TutorialText>Submit</TutorialText> and you're done! Click <TutorialText>Edit Panels</TutorialText> to see how it looks.
  </Step>
</Steps>
