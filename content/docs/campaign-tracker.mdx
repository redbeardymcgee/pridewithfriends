## Campaign Tracker

For those of you who **are** streaming, but do not want to implement your own individual campaign and overlays, you may use this overlay as a <TutorialText>Browser Source</TutorialText> in OBS! Just paste this into the <TutorialText>Browser Source</TutorialText> and set it to `1920x1080` resolution

[Overlay](https://overlays.tiltify.com/1IZ_AXFieYk0RtJOGKsXRJrXD4m7BgcS) <WarningText>WARNING:</WarningText> Old link!

This overlay shows the total progress of the **entire campaign**. It is **not suitable** for streamers who have configured their individual campaign and overlays for the event. For the rest of you who are streaming for the event but not setting up your own overlays, you can use this to let your viewers track the total progress live as they hang out with you.
